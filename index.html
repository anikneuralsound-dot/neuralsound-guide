What music separation does

Music separation takes one mixed recording and splits it into separate stems. Instead of a single locked file, you end up with parts you can mute, solo, remix or study. That is the idea behind NeuralSound AI stem separation: start with a full song, finish with the pieces.

It helps musicians learning a part, singers who want a backing track, remixers looking for clean material, and creators who need to tidy up the audio behind a video.

Pick the tool for the job

How it works

  1. Upload your file. Choose the song or recording you want to work on.
  2. Choose what to separate. Pick the tool that matches the result you need. The background music remover offers 2-, 4- and 6-track modes.
  3. Preview, then download. Listen to the separated parts first, then download the ones you want to keep.
